# TikTok Content Strategy

**Account**: @ypikaeigames
**Target Audience**: Casual gamers (18-35), indie game enthusiasts, speedrunners
**Cadence**: 3-4 posts/week during active development
**Goal**: 1000+ followers → 5000+ followers within 3 months of launch

---

## Content Mix (Weekly Breakdown)

### 60% Highlight Reels (The "WOW" Factor)

These are your viral content. Short clips of:
- **Clutch saves**: Dodger narrowly escapes destruction
- **Crazy combos**: Blocker chains 3+ destruction effects in 10 seconds
- **Epic fails**: Lag spike, unexpected mechanic interaction, bug exploit (funny but honest)
- **Speed runs**: Blockers demolish Dodgers in record time
- **Skill vs. Chaos**: High-skill play caught on a chaotic board

**Duration**: 15-45 seconds
**Audio**: Trending audio + gameplay SFX (unmute for reactions)
**Captions**: 1-2 words max ("CLUTCH" / "HOW?" / "GG" / "WAIT WHAT")
**Hook**: Show the highlight within first 3 seconds (viewers scroll fast)

**Example Scripts**:
- Dodger: *narrowly avoids block* "BRO" (chaos SFX)
- Blocker: Lands combo → destruction → both players react (energy)
- Random bug: Blocks fall in weird pattern → "IS THIS ALLOWED??" (humor)

---

### 20% Development Vlogs (Behind-the-Scenes)

Show the human side of game development:
- **Quick patches**: "We nerfed Block Spawn Rate. Here's why" (15-second explanation)
- **Feature previews**: Upcoming ability, new map teaser, cosmetic reveal
- **Bug hunts**: Developer screen-recording, "Found the sneaky bug!" montage
- **Team vibes**: Office/home setup, voice lines being recorded, sound design session
- **Community spotlight**: "This player found an exploit we didn't know about"

**Duration**: 30-60 seconds
**Audio**: Voiceover (developer explaining) + gameplay audio
**Captions**: Full subtitles (accessibility + sound-off scrolling)
**Tone**: Casual, real, occasionally self-deprecating

**Example Scripts**:
- "Our Tetris players are TOO GOOD. We had to nerf this ability."
- "This bug made blocks fall upward. It was actually pretty cool."
- "We hired a Russian voice actress. Listen to this badass voice line."

---

### 15% Memes / Humor

Gaming culture content that builds personality:
- **Game logic**: "Character pushes a block the size of a building" (physics humor)
- **Losing moments**: Rage quits, unexpected comebacks, "One more game" turns into 5
- **Skill gaps**: Casual player vs. speedrunner (side-by-side comparison)
- **Relatable gaming**: "Playing couch co-op with your friend" (funny dynamics)
- **Gaming clichés**: Parody of overused game tropes, with your game's twist

**Duration**: 15-30 seconds
**Audio**: Trending audio (meme sounds, music) + optional voiceover
**Captions**: Punchy, relatable text
**Tone**: Self-aware humor, never mean-spirited

**Example Scripts**:
- Dodger: *gets crushed* "I regret every decision that led to this moment"
- Blocker: *misses placement* "I'm a PROFESSIONAL at this game"
- "Playing I vs Blocks with your friend" — friendship instantly destroyed
- "When the new player somehow finds the unbeatable strategy"

---

### 5% Educational / Tutorial Content

Help new players understand the game:
- **How to play**: Quick 30-second intro to each role
- **Ability breakdown**: "Armor ability explained in 20 seconds"
- **Pro tips**: "Three tricks that changed my game" (speedrun tricks, combo setups)
- **Controls guide**: "Everything you need to know about dashing"
- **Beginner's guide**: Full game overview for people seeing it for the first time

**Duration**: 30-90 seconds
**Audio**: Voiceover (clear, patient tone)
**Captions**: Full subtitles + on-screen labels
**Tone**: Helpful, encouraging, non-condescending

**Example Scripts**:
- "Never played Tetris? Here's how I vs Blocks is different."
- "If you keep dying, try this one thing." (dash-jumping explanation)
- "This combo destroys 50% of the board. Here's how to pull it off."

## Viral Amplification Strategy

### Hook Optimization (First 3 Seconds)
- Open with the **action**, not the build-up
- Use trending audio immediately (algorithm favors quick engagement)
- Captions should make viewers STOP scrolling: "WAIT WHAT?", "HOW?", "THAT'S BROKEN"

### Cross-Promotion
- **YouTube Shorts**: Repurpose TikTok clips (same upload, different aspect ratio)
- **Instagram Reels**: Same content, Instagram-native hashtags
- **Discord**: Post highlight clips in announcement channel (links back to TikTok)
- **Itch.io Blog**: "Watch the best plays" embed TikTok videos

### Hashtag Strategy
**Primary Hashtags** (use in every post):
- #IndieGames #GameDev #GamingCommunity #AsymmetricGaming #Multiplayer

**Secondary Hashtags** (rotate):
- #TetrisLike #PuzzleGame #LocalCoop #OnlineGaming #IndieGamedev

**Trending Hashtags** (use when applicable):
- #FYP #ForYouPage #Viral #GameClips #eSports

**Bilingual Hashtags** (reach Russian audience):
- #ИндиИгры #Геймдев #МультиплеерИгры

**Total**: 15-20 hashtags per post

### Engagement Strategy

**Respond to Every Comment (Within 24 Hours)**
- "How did you do that?" → "It's the Dash combo! Check this tutorial video"
- "This looks fun" → "Download and challenge your friend! Here's the link"
- Negative comments → "We appreciate the feedback. Here's why we made that choice"
- Bug reports → "Oh no! We're fixing this in the next patch. Thanks for reporting!"

**Pin Top Comments**
- Pin a comment that asks questions ("How do I unlock that ability?")
- Pin positive reactions ("This is insane!")
- Don't pin self-promotional comments (looks spammy)

**Create Duets & Stitches**
- Invite popular gaming creators to play your game
- React to fan clips (boost community creators)
- Collaborate with other indie dev accounts (cross-pollination)

## Metrics to Track

### Health Metrics (Weekly)
- **Followers**: Absolute count + week-over-week growth rate
- **Engagement Rate**: (Likes + Comments + Shares) / Followers
- **Watch Time**: Average seconds watched per video
- **CTR to itch.io**: How many views converted to page visits (use URL tracking)

### Content Performance (Weekly)
- **Highlight Reels**: Track which styles get most engagement (clutch vs. combo vs. speed)
- **Dev Vlogs**: Watch time tells you what topics people care about
- **Memes**: Comment volume and sentiment (positive laughter vs. confused reactions)
- **Educational**: Conversion to tutorials watched (if linked)

### Audience Demographics (Monthly)
- TikTok Analytics: Age, region, gender distribution
- Top countries: Identify if EN or RU content dominates
- Follower growth: Are followers staying? (engagement vs. follower ratio)
- Traffic source: Where are new followers coming from? (other creators, hashtags, FYP)

## Crisis & Controversy Management

**If a gameplay clip shows a bug or exploit:**
- Post alongside: "We're fixing this bug in the next patch"
- Frame as: "Accidentally breaking the game like..." (self-aware humor)
- Don't hide the problem (players know, transparency builds trust)

**If someone reports a serious issue in comments:**
- Respond publicly: "DM us, we want to fix this ASAP"
- Follow up with DM (show you care)
- Post update later: "Thanks for reporting. Fixed in [patch]"

**If balance change is controversial:**
- Post dev blog explaining the reasoning
- Respond to top critical comments (don't be defensive)
- Post data: "Here's why we made this change" (show win rates, playtime)

## Red Flags to Avoid

- **Posting too much**: 5+ posts/day = follower fatigue. Stick to 3-4/week
- **Dead days**: If you post, be consistent. Gaps kill momentum
- **Low-effort clips**: Blurry gameplay, no captions, stolen audio = looks unprofessional
- **Ignore comments**: Respond to EVERY comment in first 24 hours
- **Clickbait without substance**: "SHOCKING GAMEPLAY" must deliver on the promise
- **Negative tone toward players**: Never mock players, even bad ones
- **Endless promotion**: Mix in fun content. Every post can't be "Download now"
