## Welcome to I vs Blocks Community

We're building a community where players of all skill levels feel safe, respected, and excited to play together. These guidelines help us maintain that space.

**In short**: Be respectful, have fun, and remember there's a person on the other side of the screen.

---

## Community Values

### 1. Respect

Treat everyone as you'd like to be treated.
- **Respect player skill levels**: New players and pros play together. Welcome both.
- **Respect your opponent**: Winning or losing with grace builds respect.
- **Respect the developers**: We're a small team doing our best. Feedback welcome; personal attacks aren't.
- **Respect different playstyles**: Some play casually, some competitively. Both are valid.

### 2. Inclusivity

Everyone belongs here—regardless of background, skill level, language, or identity.
- **No discrimination**: Racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, ableism = automatic removal
- **Language accessibility**: We support EN and RU. Help newer players learn the game.
- **Skill diversity**: Celebrate speedrunners AND casual players. We need both.

### 3. Constructive Feedback

Balance suggestions? Bug reports? We want to hear it. But HOW you say it matters.

**Good feedback**:
- "I think Block Spawn Rate is too fast because games end in 2 minutes"
- "I found a bug where [specific steps to reproduce]"
- "Could we get a colorblind mode? Here's why it would help me"

**Bad feedback**:
- "This game is trash and you suck"
- "Nerf [ability] NOW or I'm leaving"
- "[Developer name] is an idiot"

## Prohibited Behavior

The following will result in warnings, mutes, or bans (see escalation below):

### Hate Speech & Harassment
- Slurs or derogatory language targeting any group (race, gender, sexuality, disability, etc.)
- Personal attacks or doxxing (sharing someone's real-world info)
- Harassment campaigns ("spam everyone about this player's bad play")
- Repeated unwanted contact after someone asks you to stop

### Cheating & Exploits
- Using bugs intentionally in ranked matches (known exploits = auto-disconnect)
- Account boosting (paying someone to play your account for you)
- Lag switching or network manipulation
- Discussing/sharing exploit tutorials (report instead)

### Spam & Self-Promotion
- Posting the same message 3+ times in a row
- Advertising other games/products without context ("go play [game]")
- Self-promoting without adding value to the conversation
- Recruiting for MLMs, crypto schemes, etc.

**Exception**: Sharing your own fan art, speedruns, or community content is encouraged (not spam)

### Sexual Content & NSFW
- Sexually explicit images, links, or descriptions
- NSFW roleplay in community spaces
- Unwanted sexual advances
- Impersonation (catfishing, fake accounts pretending to be someone else)

### Misinformation
- Deliberately spreading false information about the game ("this ability is banned")
- Conspiracy theories unrelated to the game
- Spoilers in non-spoiler channels (mark them clearly: `[SPOILER] ...`)

### Toxicity
- Excessive negativity ("this game will die", "you all suck")
- Rage quitting with insulting messages
- Taunting opponents after wins
- Mocking players for their skill level

## What's Encouraged

### Great Community Contributions

**Fan Content**
- Fan art (character, story, cosmetics you'd like to see)
- Speedrun clips (share your best times)
- Tutorials & guides (help new players learn)
- Meme content (self-aware humor about the game)
- Music remixes (game soundtrack covers or original)

**Constructive Engagement**
- Detailed balance feedback ("here's data on why X is too strong")
- Bug reports with reproduction steps (huge help to devs)
- Feature suggestions with use cases ("I'd like X because Y")
- Competitive play analysis ("here's a strategy I discovered")
- Lore discussions (theories, character interpretations)

**Community Building**
- Helping new players learn the game
- Organizing community events (tournaments, challenges)
- Celebrating other players' achievements
- Translating community content (EN ↔ RU)
- Moderator volunteering (apply in announcements channel)

## Enforcement: Escalation Process

We use graduated responses. Context matters—no one-strike-you're-out policy.

### Level 1: Verbal Warning
**When**: First minor offense (spam, mild toxicity, accidental rule break)
**How**: Public comment or private DM
**Example**: "Hey, please limit posts to once per channel. Thanks!"
**Outcome**: Warning on record. Next offense = Level 2

### Level 2: Temporary Mute (24 Hours)
**When**: Repeated Level 1 offense, or more serious single offense (hate speech, personal attack)
**How**: User cannot post in community for 24 hours
**Record**: Logged (multiple mutes → ban)
**Example**: Racism, targeted harassment, explicit spoilers without warning

### Level 3: Extended Mute (1 Week)
**When**: Multiple Level 2 mutes, or very serious single offense (doxxing, sexual harassment)
**How**: User cannot post for 7 days
**Appeal**: User can appeal to moderation team with explanation
**Record**: Strong negative record toward permanent ban

### Level 4: Permanent Ban
**When**: Multiple extended mutes, or egregious behavior (organized harassment, slurs in every post, etc.)
**How**: User removed from community entirely, unable to create new accounts with same email
**Appeal Process**: User can email [community@ypikaeigames.com] with explanation. Reviewed by leadership after 30 days (usually denied, but possible if genuine apology)

### Emergency Removal (Immediate)
**When**: Illegal activity, severe NSFW content, credible threats
**How**: Instant permanent ban without warning
**Appeal**: Not available (serious offenses only)

## Moderation Transparency

### What Moderators Do
- Monitor channels for rule violations
- Issue warnings and enforce mutes/bans consistently
- Respond to reports within 24 hours
- Keep records of all actions (for consistency reviews)
- Escalate complex cases to community manager

### What Moderators Don't Do
- Ban people for disagreeing with the game's design
- Mute constructive criticism (even if it stings)
- Show favoritism (no special treatment for streamers, friends, etc.)
- Delete posts unless they violate rules (we keep records)

### Appealing a Decision
**Mute or Ban**: Email [community@ypikaeigames.com]
- Include: Your username, what happened, why you think it was wrong
- Wait: We review appeals within 7 days
- Be honest: "I didn't know that was against the rules" is better than arguing

**Moderator Behavior**: If a moderator violates these guidelines (harassing you, showing bias), report to [community@ypikaeigames.com] with evidence. Leadership will investigate.

## Special Cases & Clarifications

### Competitive Trash Talk
**Context matters**: "Your playstyle is predictable" ≠ "You're an idiot"
- Talking about gameplay: OK
- Attacking the person: Not OK

**Rule of thumb**: Would you say this if you were grabbing coffee with the other player? If not, don't type it.

### Language & Accents
We don't allow mockery of accents, languages, or ESL players. Everyone learns English and Russian at their own pace.

**OK**: Answering ESL player's grammar question
**Not OK**: "Lol, how are you so bad at English?"

### Mental Health Discussions
We care about player wellbeing. Topics like depression, anxiety, etc. are OK here—but:
- No unsolicited mental health "advice" (you're not a therapist)
- If someone is in crisis, point them to professional resources
- Be kind; mental health discussions aren't "toxicity"

### Paid Sponsorships & Streamers
If you're a streamer or content creator with a sponsorship deal:
- Disclose it in your posts ("Sponsored content" tag)
- Don't pretend the game is better than it is for payment
- Same rules apply (no hate speech, harassment, etc.)

## Reporting Violations

### How to Report
1. **Discord/Forum**: Use the report button (three-dot menu → Report)
2. **Email**: [community@ypikaeigames.com] with username and reason
3. **Moderation Panel**: DM a moderator directly (response in 24 hours)

### What to Include
- **Who**: Username of the person violating rules
- **What**: What did they do/say?
- **When**: Date and time (if possible)
- **Screenshot**: If you can, grab a screenshot as evidence
- **Why**: Which rule(s) were violated?

**Example Good Report**:
```
Username: PlayerX123
Violation: Homophobic slur in #competitive channel
Time: March 25, 2:15 PM (UTC+3)
Screenshot: [attached]
Rule: "Hate Speech & Harassment"
```

### False Reports
**Don't report just because you disagree with someone's opinion.** False reports waste moderator time. Repeated false reports = mute.

**OK to report**: "This person called me [slur]"
**Not OK to report**: "I don't like their balance opinions"
