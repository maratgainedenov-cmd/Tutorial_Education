# Marketing Strategy: I vs Blocks

**Game**: I vs Blocks (BLOKS vs CHARACTER в рабочих материалах)
**Platforms**: itch.io (primary), TikTok, YouTube (secondary)
**Regions**: RU + EN (bilingual strategy)
**Target Audience**: Casual puzzle gamers, indie game enthusiasts, speedrunners (niche)
**Status**: Pre-release, beta testing phase

---

## Market Position

**Genre**: Local multiplayer puzzle battler with narrative depth (vs. pure casual puzzle)
**Unique Selling Points**:
1. Asymmetric gameplay (Tetris-style blocker vs. character dodger)
2. Couch co-op and online multiplayer (via Photon PUN2)
3. Dark comedy tone with lore-driven narrative
4. Skill-based combat system with buyable abilities
5. Free-to-play with cosmetics (monetization planned)

**Competitive Advantages**:
- Fills niche between casual puzzlers and competitive multiplayer games
- Accessible controls + deep tactical play
- Bilingual (EN/RU) from day one
- Community-driven balance iteration

## Bilingual Strategy (EN/RU)

**Messaging**:
- All patch notes published in both languages simultaneously
- Dev blogs: English primary, Russian translation within 48 hours
- Social media: Alternate posts EN/RU or dual-language captions
- Community: Moderation team includes Russian-speaking members

**Cultural Localization**:
- Respect regional humor (avoid culturally insensitive jokes)
- Use local gaming terminology (e.g., Russian speedrun community preferences)
- Feature Russian streamers/content creators in spotlights

## Community Health

### Moderation
- **Guidelines**: Clear, published, enforced consistently
- **Escalation**: Warning → mute → ban (document all actions)
- **Tone**: Friendly, non-adversarial (even with rule-breakers)

### Engagement
- **Response SLA**: Acknowledge feedback within 24 hours
- **Feature Requests**: Track, categorize, share monthly summary
- **Bug Reports**: Triage, assign severity, public status updates
- **Close the Loop**: "You asked for X → we delivered in patch Y" posts

## Crisis Communication Plan

**Outage / Game-Breaking Bug**:
1. Acknowledge on itch.io within 30 minutes
2. Post hourly status updates on TikTok/Discord
3. Estimate fix time (or "investigating")
4. Deploy fix, post patch notes
5. Post-mortem blog explaining what happened + prevention steps

**Controversial Balance Change**:
1. Explain rationale before patch (dev blog)
2. Publish detailed patch notes with before/after numbers
3. Monitor feedback for 48 hours
4. Respond to top 5-10 comments addressing concerns
5. If overwhelmingly wrong, plan hotfix (communicate ETA)

## Monetization & Sustainability

**Current Model**: Free-to-play on itch.io (no paywalls)
**Future Monetization** (Month 4+, subject to design approval):
- Cosmetics: Character skins, block skins, VFX packs
- Battle Pass: Seasonal rewards (cosmetics + cosmetic currency)
- Premium cosmetics: Limited-edition cross-promotional items

**Community Messaging**:
- Transparency: "We're a small team, cosmetics keep us going"
- Fair play: "Cosmetics don't affect gameplay"
- Value: "Each cosmetic tells a story (lore tie-ins, community voting)"

---

## Success Criteria (3 Months Post-Launch)

- **Player Base**: 500+ monthly active users, 50+ concurrent during peak hours
- **Engagement**: Average session > 20 minutes, D30 retention > 30%
- **Community**: 200+ social media followers, 50+ regular community contributors
- **Sentiment**: 75%+ positive feedback, <5% toxic incidents
- **Monetization Readiness**: Cosmetics pipeline designed, first 5 skins ready for launch
